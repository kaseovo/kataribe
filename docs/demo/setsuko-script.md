# 節子さんの台本: her two conversations, as voiced

Both conversations of the demo persona, [小川節子](persona.md), line for line as they were voiced and uploaded. The video uses the lines marked in its last column; the rest are only in her recordings, where her family page's quotes come from.

## Session 1

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Kataribe | 節子さん、こんにちは。今日は、郡上の若いころのお話、聞かせてくださいね。 | Hello, Setsuko. Today, please tell me about your young days in Gujo. | Cold open |
| s1 | Setsuko | はい、こんにちは。若いころねえ……やっぱり、郡上おどりかしらねえ。 | Hello. My young days… the Gujo Odori, I suppose. | Cold open |
| 06 | Kataribe | 郡上おどり、いいですねえ。どなたと踊られたんですか。 | The Gujo Odori, how lovely. Who did you dance with? | Session 1 |

| 08 | Kataribe | まあ、和夫さんと。その晩は、何を踊っていらしたんですか。 | Oh, with Kazuo. What were you dancing that night? | Session 1 |

| 13 | Kataribe | 新橋から。どのくらいの高さがあったんですか。 | From the Shinbashi bridge. How high was it? | Session 1 |

| s10 | Setsuko | こちらこそ、ありがとう。楽しかったわ。 | Thank you too. I enjoyed it. | |

## Between the sessions
