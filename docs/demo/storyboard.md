# Storyboard

The target cut, second by second, for [demo-script.md](demo-script.md). It is imagined. Setsuko's lines are those in [setsuko-script.md](setsuko-script.md), the interviewer's are predictions from its prompt (`interviewer_ja.md`), and Tanguy's in *Your turn* are placeholders for whatever Tanguy really says. The take will say something close, not the same. In the edit, use the nearest real moment and keep the timings. Burned-in subtitles carry a speaker label; narration captions are in italic.

The times are measured, not guessed. An animatic voiced every line at a natural pace and timed each moment to its line, and the whole runs 3:43, inside the 3:00–4:00 window.
